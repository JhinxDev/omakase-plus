# Understand the interface

**Key saved securely** means a credential is already stored on the server.
Choose **Change key** to enter a replacement, or **Cancel** to keep the saved key.
**Remove key** is a separate action. Connection status describes the last check;
having a saved key alone does not mean the service is reachable.

Search hints and results align with the field. On a phone, the catalog selector
and search field stack so titles have room. Use the arrow keys to choose a
suggestion, Enter to open it, or Escape to close it.

A service warning gives its saved cause and a next step. Open the linked settings
to review the connection or source. A temporary failed source check does not mean
your media has been deleted. Host-managed sources include operator setup guidance.

History shows an episode image only when Omakase can match the exact episode.
Otherwise an episode-number placeholder says **Image unavailable**. Movies keep
their movie artwork. Opening History does not change your progress or ratings.
