# TV shows

TV Shows sits alongside Anime and Movies. On a phone, choose Browse to open those
three libraries. Search also offers a TV Shows category with catalog suggestions.

Connect Plex, Seerr and the configured media backend in Settings. Use a unique
Plex library named **TV Shows**. Import the collection through its explicit sync
action, then open a show and choose **Check in Plex** to read exact season/episode
inventory and recorded episode watch flags. Imported watch flags never replace
your manual episode corrections. No AniList updates are made for general TV.

A catalog listing does not mean that you have a file. In Plex is exact library
inventory; this TV section does not label it as byte-verified Ready. Open in Plex
uses the matched series identity. Check again after the Plex server or its content
changes. Source-account changes invalidate prior observations.

Choose a season to browse episodes. Load or refresh its catalog listing to pick up
new entries; actual air dates determine whether marking watched is offered. An
unknown or future date cannot be marked watched manually. Save a series status
and rating independently; this does not mark every episode watched. History shows
explicit episode/completion records and their source. Observation dates are not
claimed as the time you watched them.

Automatic TV acquisition is not enabled in this candidate. The installed legacy
Riven adapter uses administrator-managed season scopes; a whole-series request
would not safely represent a selected-season request. Keep existing Riven/Plex
TV media intact and use the operator workflow for additions until a documented,
independently qualified season request interface is implemented.

The collection uses explicit pages rather than silently dropping older shows.
The Plex inventory import/check is bounded and fails visibly on incomplete or
ambiguous identity data. It never falls back to guessing from a title string.
