# Notifications

The bell beside Account keeps your saved rating and media reminders together. Its badge shows unread reminders after one short local check. Open it to see what needs attention; an unavailable check stays clearly labeled rather than showing a false zero.

| Reminder | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Completed anime, movie or TV series without a rating | Choose **Rate title** to open its existing rating/progress section. TV reminders use your explicit series-completed record. |
| Completed managed anime with a saved media-review reminder | See your current rating and choose **Review media**. This opens the existing confirmation flow; the bell never removes anything. |

A media-review reminder is limited to anime for which Omakase has a saved safe ownership model. Shared or adopted assets remain protected. Movie and TV cleanup reminders are not offered until those workflows have their own safe ownership model. A saved reminder does not claim a fresh provider or Plex check.

Opening **Rate title** or **Review media** marks that reminder as read without delaying navigation. **Dismiss** (the small cross) hides it for your account. These notification actions do not change ratings, watch progress or media. A genuine later completion can create a new reminder. Other accounts' reminders are separate.

Close the panel, press Escape or click outside to continue. The list scrolls within the screen on phones. If a request fails, use **Try again** or refresh/sign in when the message requests it. Missing optional services do not prevent ordinary rating reminders.

The header makes one deferred local check per page and reuses it when opened. It does not poll, import history, contact providers, run paid inference, acquire media or start removal. Posters appear beside each reminder when artwork is available. Images start loading only when you open the list, through the same artwork cache used elsewhere in Omakase. A cache miss may make its normal bounded metadata/CDN request; missing artwork keeps a neutral fallback.
